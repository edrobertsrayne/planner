Placing a Lesson outside a Topic onto one Class

PROTOTYPE — throwaway. Question (issue #225): how does a Lesson with no Topic reach one Class's schedule, on one chosen date, without breaking the one derived function? ADR-0007 says the schedule is a pure zip of Lessons against Available Slots, with no move, shift or insert, so a placement has to be a change to the input, the way a Blocked Slot is. This page runs three candidate mechanisms side by side over the real 2026/27 calendar and one real Class (9B/Sc1, five Periods a fortnight including the Thursday double), so you can watch which of them tells the truth and which one loses a Lesson.

The Class teaches one Topic, Forces, of eight Lessons. Two Lessons stand outside every Topic and are the ones you place: Revision (Length 1) and Feedback (Length 2).
