# Printable views

The Agenda and the Calendar have no Print control, no print stylesheet and no PDF route.

## Why this is out of scope

The need is rare: a paper copy for a cover folder, an observation or a day with no screen, a few
times a year. A print layout is not a one-off cost. Each view that has one must keep its print
layout correct every time the screen changes: new rows, Open Slots, Blocked Slots, Readiness,
Tones. That upkeep continues after the feature ships.

For the rare day that needs paper, the browser can print the screen as it is. Or the teacher can
copy the plan out of the Lesson editor.
